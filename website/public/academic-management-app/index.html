Academic information stays connected

Academic management works best when timetable, attendance, exams, and student records are not handled in separate tools. Akademifyy keeps these workflows aligned for school teams.

For admins

Configure academic years, classes, subjects, timetable, exams, attendance settings, and reports.

For teachers

Access assigned classes, students, timetable, attendance tools, study material, and exam activities.

For parents

View attendance, timetable, notices, exam schedules, results, study materials, and fee status.

Akademifyy is designed for repeated school routines, not just one-time setup. Schools can use it to track classes, academic records, communication, and reports over time.
