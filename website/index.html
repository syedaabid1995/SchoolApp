Features

Everything your school needs to run smoothly

From admissions to alumni, Akademify covers the full lifecycle of school operations — in one connected system.

Student records

Every student's file in one place — enrollment details, documents, academic history, and emergency contacts, always up to date.

Smart attendance

Mark attendance in seconds and let Akademify handle the rest — automatic alerts to parents the moment a student is marked absent.

Grades & report cards

Set your own grading scale, let teachers enter marks from anywhere, and generate report cards your school will be proud to send home.

Fee management

Collect fees online, send gentle reminders automatically, and know exactly who's paid — and who hasn't — at a glance.

Timetable & scheduling

Build a conflict-free timetable for every class and teacher in minutes, with drag-and-drop scheduling that adjusts as things change.

Parent & teacher portal

Keep families in the loop with real-time updates, announcements, and direct messaging — no more notes lost in a backpack.

Staff & HR

Manage contracts, leave requests, and performance reviews for your whole staff, from the front office to the classroom.

Reports & analytics

Turn attendance, grades, and fee data into clear dashboards, so you always know how your school is really doing.
