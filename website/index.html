Yes. Modules, fields, reports, fee structures, roles, and workflows can be customized based on your school’s process.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. This website and the product interface are designed to work smoothly on desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

Can parents receive notifications?

Yes. Akademify can support announcements, attendance updates, homework, fee reminders, exam schedules, and result notifications.

Can we migrate old student records?

Yes. Existing records can be imported using Excel or CSV templates during setup.
